Word → Brightspace

Turn a .docx into formatted text for a Brightspace text-submission box. Your file never leaves this browser: no upload, no AI, no tracking.

Formatting

How to paste
  1. Click Copy for Brightspace.
  2. In Brightspace, open the assignment and click inside the text-submission box.
  3. Paste with Ctrl+V (⌘+V on Mac). Don't use "paste as plain text" (Ctrl+Shift+V), which removes formatting.
  4. If Brightspace asks how to paste, choose to keep formatting.
  5. Scroll through the pasted text and compare it with this preview before you submit.

Before you submit: preview the pasted result in Brightspace and check it carefully. Follow your instructor's submission rules. If they ask for an attached file, attach the file instead.
